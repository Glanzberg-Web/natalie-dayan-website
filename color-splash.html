Discover Color Splash

A whole lot of fun, with purpose behind the play.

I began Color Splash in Jacksonville, Florida, in 2015 to bring children a sensory-rich space to explore and learn.

Classes introduce vocabulary, opportunities for socialization, and cognitive development through tactile experiences. As a speech-language pathologist, I guide playful communication using multiple ways of learning, with a focus on the whole child.
