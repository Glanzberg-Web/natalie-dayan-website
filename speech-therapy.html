My approach

Real life is the best starting point.

I incorporate the things that already exist in a client's life—family, toys, friendships, schoolwork and hobbies—to work toward their goals.

My experience spans evaluations and treatment from infancy through adulthood across homes, schools, hospitals, nursing homes and private practices. Each setting deepened my appreciation for individual strengths and evolving communication needs.

Care is grounded in supportive relationships, meaningful progress, and celebrating every achievement.
