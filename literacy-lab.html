About Literacy Lab

Learning to read begins long before a book is opened.

Developed in 2025, Literacy Lab is a playful, multisensory early-literacy experience built around storytelling and language foundations.

Through art, science, and sensory play, children explore the building blocks of stories and reading readiness. Each week invites curiosity, conversation, experimentation, and creative expression.

It can get a little messy—and that's part of the magic.
