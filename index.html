A little about me

Hi! I'm Natalie.

I'm a speech-language pathologist, mom, wife, homeschool pod teacher and tutor, and the creator of Color Splash and Literacy Lab.

My passion is helping children communicate, connect, and grow. I bring the people and things children already love—family, friends, toys, schoolwork, and hobbies—into each learning experience.
