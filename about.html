My story

Hi! I'm Natalie Dayan.

I have a passion for working with children of all ages and finding the spark that makes learning meaningful.

My goal is to bring what already exists in each child's world—family, friendships, play, hobbies, and schoolwork—into the process of building skills, improving communication, and experiencing growth and success.

I've worked as a speech-language pathologist in homes, schools, hospitals, nursing homes, and private practices, serving people from infancy through adulthood. Every setting taught me more about the unique ways people connect, develop, and learn.

I'm also the creator of Color Splash, which began in 2015, and Literacy Lab, developed in 2025, and I work as a homeschool pod teacher and tutor.
